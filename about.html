About Media Pig AF

Media Pig AF is an independent music production and artist development company based in Nashville, Tennessee. It creates original recordings, develops artists, and connects finished work with new opportunities.

The company was founded on decades of experience in music production and collaboration, and it holds to authenticity, artistic independence, and quality.

The goal is to help independent artists extend the creative and commercial life of their recordings beyond a single release cycle.

Services

  • Music production
  • Artist development
  • Live performance recording
  • Original media
  • Sync licensing representation
